Slime Kingdom Clicker - Windows portable build

Double-click Slime-Kingdom-Clicker.exe to launch the game.
This build includes the Electron desktop shell and AI companion fallback.
For live local AI and voice, place the Qwen .gguf and Piper .onnx files in the MODELS folder beside the executable, together with platform-native runtime binaries when rebuilding.
See DESKTOP_BUILD.md for the complete build and runtime instructions.
